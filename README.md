Bouncy Bow!

This is our game Bouncy Bow, where you shoot you shoot your enemies with arrows as they arrive in waves! You will be met with a ultimate boss fight to beat the game.


Use WASD to move around and left click to shoot. To kill your enemies you must bounce your arrows off walls.
